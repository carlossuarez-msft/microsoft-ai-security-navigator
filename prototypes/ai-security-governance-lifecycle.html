Microsoft product roles by scenario

Customers get confused because several portals participate. This section names the control plane role in plain language.

Do not ask, “which product secures AI?” Ask, “which control plane leads for this risk?”

For example, Purview leads data security, Entra leads identity and agent access, Defender leads discovery and detection, Intune enforces device posture, Foundry secures custom AI apps, and Sentinel/Security Copilot operationalize the SOC response.

The Microsoft AI security operating model

Read this as a sequence, not a product list: secure identity first, understand data exposure, govern apps and agents, protect the AI runtime, then operationalize detection and reporting.

The simple message for leaders

Microsoft does not secure AI through one console. It secures AI through connected control planes. Each plane answers a leadership question: who can act, what data can be used, which agents exist, how the AI workload is protected, and how the SOC responds.
